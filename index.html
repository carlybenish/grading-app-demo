Discussion Grades

TAs grade discussion sections on their phones. Each grade lands in a Google Sheet the moment it’s entered, every change is logged, and the sheet turns any class day into a Canvas import file. No paper sheets, and no retyping.

Try it

  • Tap an empty grade box. It fills in 10. Tap the next student and the 10 is saved: a green ✓ appears in the app and the grade shows up in the spreadsheet below.
  • Type any other grade from 0 to 10, or use Same for all to grade a whole group at once.
  • Tap Late next to a name, then pick how late they were. Their box fills in that lateness’s max (8 at 5 minutes, down to 4 at 25 or more) instead of 10.
  • Tap All at the top of the app to see how far along each section is.
  • In the spreadsheet, choose Canvas ▾ to turn the day into a Canvas import.

    How it works

    For TAs

    One link, on a phone or a laptop. It opens on today’s class with students grouped by table, and shows which TA covers which tables. TAs don’t need access to the spreadsheet or an app to install.

    Only a dark number with a green ✓ is saved. If a grade hasn’t reached the spreadsheet, a red bar appears and the app keeps retrying. Unsaved grades stay on the phone and are sent the next time the link is opened there.

    In the spreadsheet

    The Roster tab is the student list: section, table, group, Canvas IDs, and TA assignments. Edit it and the app picks up the change within a minute.

    Each class day gets its own tab the first time anyone grades that day. The Change log records every change (old grade, new grade, who, when), so an overwritten grade can always be recovered. A grade fixed in the sheet reaches the app within about 20 seconds.

    Into Canvas

    The sheet’s Canvas menu turns a day’s tab into a file for Canvas’s Grades → Import. Students are matched by Canvas ID, not by name.

    Each class day is tied to an assignment that already exists in Canvas, and the import never creates a new one. Students with no grade are left out, so Canvas doesn’t enter zeros.

    Setting it up

    It’s a Google Sheet with a small Apps Script web app attached. You paste in two files and deploy it, which takes about 10 minutes, with no server and nothing to pay for. Deploy it so only people at your school can open it, and the grades stay in your own Google account.

    It was built for one course: four discussion sections that meet Wednesdays and Fridays, graded 0–10. To use it for another course, you’d edit the section list, semester dates, and Canvas assignment list in the code. Ask whoever shared this demo for the files and the setup guide.
